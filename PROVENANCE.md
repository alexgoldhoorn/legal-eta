# Provenance

This library was written from public sources only:

- Regulation (EC) No 561/2006 and Regulation (EU) 2020/1054 (text as published).
- The European Commission's guidance notes 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7 on driving times and rest periods.
- Public academic literature on driver scheduling.

No code, documents, data or designs from any employer or client were used.
The author's professional experience in freight planning informed only the
observation that this tool is missing, not its contents.

Planning aid, not legal advice.
